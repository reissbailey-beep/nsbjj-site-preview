Quick questions

Do I need a gi?

For most classes, yes. Most of our timetable is gi; only a few classes are no-gi. You get a free loaner gi for your first week. After that, a loaner is £10 a class.

What if I'm between sizes?

That's why we fit you in person. We try different gis on you at your first class and go with the best fit.

Can I buy a gi later?

Yes, ask at the front desk. Adding it now is one tap on the same card, and we fit it at your first class.

Is it the same as the lightweight gi?

No. The lightweight gi isn't the same quality, and you can't compete in it. This is our premium gi: competition-ready, with the Brixton Team patch.
