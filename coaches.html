Owner & coach · Black belt

Reiss Bailey

“Jiu-jitsu is more than just a martial art. It's a transformative journey that teaches discipline, resilience and valuable life lessons.”

Reiss opened New School on Brixton Hill in November 2022 with one idea: build a gym where a nervous first-timer and a world-level competitor feel equally at home. He still teaches the Beginners classes himself, so the coach who welcomes you on day one is the owner.

He knows what it takes to keep showing up. He won his first world title as a brown belt, was promoted to black belt in front of the whole team in December 2024, then went back to the World Masters and won it again, at black belt. What he teaches you has been tested where it matters most.

Head coach · Black belt

Leo Neves

Leo stepped onto the mats in Brazil at four years old and never really stepped off.

Jiu-jitsu has been his whole life. In 2023 he became AJP World Pro champion at brown belt in Abu Dhabi. He earned his black belt in 2024. In 2025 he moved to London and made New School his home, and in 2026 he took gold at black belt at the AJP Grand Slam in Moscow.

Now he leads our 7am Fundamentals and weekday Advanced classes. He's at his best breaking big moves into small details you can actually use, whether it's your first month or your tenth year.
