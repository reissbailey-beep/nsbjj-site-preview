What they get

More than
a sport.

Jiu-jitsu is problem-solving with your whole body. Kids learn to stay calm, think under pressure and keep trying, and have a lot of fun doing it.

01

Confidence

Every new skill they learn is proof they can do hard things, on the mats and off them.

02

Discipline

Listening, taking turns, following instructions and trying again after a mistake. Habits that last.

03

Fitness

Strength, balance, coordination and flexibility, from an hour on the mats that feels like play.

04

Standing up to bullying

BJJ teaches kids to stay calm and in control without punching or kicking, and gives them the confidence to stand tall.

05

Friendships

The same faces every week, working together and helping each other. Friendships grow on the mats.

06

For parents

Coached, structured classes at times that fit around school, and a healthy way to burn off energy.

FAQ

Parents
ask.

Anything else? Call or WhatsApp 07578 284838.

What ages do you teach?

Cubs are 4–7 and Juniors are 8–12. Each group trains separately.

Does my child need any experience?

No. Most kids start with none at all. The first class is free so they can try it before you decide anything.

What should they wear to the free class?

Comfy sportswear is fine, no kit needed. Bring a bottle of water. If they join the Intro Programme, their gi is included.

How much does it cost after the free class?

The 4-month Kids Intro Programme is £325 in total: £100 for the first month (gi included), then £75 a month, or £300 if you pay in full. Brothers and sisters get 20% off. See all prices.

What if we stop early?

The Intro Programme is a 4-month commitment. If you're paying monthly and leave early, at any point, there's a £150 charge. Paid in full? No charge.

What happens after the four months?

Your child moves up to the Advanced Kids Programme, at the same monthly price.

How do I book the free class?

Book it online: trial classes run Monday, Wednesday and Thursday, at 4:15pm for Cubs and 5:00pm for Juniors.
