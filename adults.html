Your first class

What to
expect.

Every class follows the same rhythm, so you'll know what's coming. You'll never be forced to do anything you don't want to, and you're welcome to watch your first session if you'd prefer.

  1. Warm-upWe get moving together as a group, with simple drills that build the movements you'll use.
  2. TechniqueThe coach shows the technique of the day and breaks it down step by step.
  3. DrillingYou practise it in pairs. Your partner lets you complete the move without resisting.
  4. Light rollingSparring at the end, at your own pace. Go light, or sit out and watch. Your call.
Beginners' classes

Built for
day one.

Dedicated beginners' classes run every week, with classes in the mornings, at lunchtime and in the evenings. You'll learn the fundamentals alongside people at the same stage, and move on when you're ready.

  • Most of our classes are in the gi, with a few no-gi classes.
  • We lend you a gi for your first week. After that, a loaner is £10 a class, or get your own.
  • Wear a t-shirt or rash guard under the gi. Bring water and flip-flops.
  • Book classes in the Gymdesk app. Arrive 10 minutes early for your first one.

I've never trained. Can I start?

Yes. Our beginners' classes are made for complete beginners. You'll warm up as a group, learn the technique of the day, drill it with a partner and roll lightly at the end, at your own pace.

Do I need to be fit first?

No. You get fit by training, not before. Work at your own pace and take a break whenever you need one.

Do I have to spar?

Want to spar in week one? You can, when you're ready. Not ready? Watch or sit out. Tap, and it stops. You'll never be forced to do anything you don't want to.

Am I too old?

Almost certainly not. Coach Reiss started at 25 with no sports background, and both of his world titles came in the Masters divisions, for athletes over 30. You train at your own pace, rest whenever you need to and spar only when you're ready. [Placeholder: members who started in their 40s or later, with permission]

What do I wear?

Most of our classes are in the gi. We lend you one for your first week; after that, a loaner is £10 a class, or you can get your own. Wear a t-shirt or rash guard underneath, keep your nails short, and bring water and flip-flops.
