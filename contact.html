Get directions
  • Bus
    Stops right outside

    Routes 45, 59, 109, 133, 159, 250 and 333 run up Brixton Hill. Get off at Jebb Avenue / Brixton Prison or Blenheim Gardens.

  • Tube
    Brixton (Victoria line)

    About a 15-minute walk straight up Brixton Hill, or a few stops on any of the buses above. Brixton rail station is next to it.

  • Car
    Parking

    There's no parking on Brixton Hill itself. You can park on some nearby side streets at certain times: check the local parking signs for controlled hours before you leave your car.
