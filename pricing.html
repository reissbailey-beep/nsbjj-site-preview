What happens after my trial month?

Your membership continues at [Placeholder: roll-on price awaiting Reiss — £70 a month (2 classes a week) or £95 unlimited] unless you cancel. Membership options are £70 a month for 2 classes a week or £95 a month unlimited.

How do I cancel?

Any time, with no notice period and no fee. Message us in Gymdesk, email us or tell us at the gym, and no further payments will be taken.

Do I need to buy a gi?

Not straight away. We lend you a gi for your first week. After that, a loaner is £10 a class, or you can get your own.

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee to start. If you cancel your membership and later re-join, there's a £75 re-joining fee.

What if my child leaves the Intro Programme early?

It's a 4-month commitment. If you're paying monthly, leaving early at any point costs £150. If you paid in full, there's no charge.

Do brothers and sisters get a discount?

Yes: 20% off for each brother or sister on the kids programme.
