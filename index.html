FAQ

Good
questions.

Anything else? Call 07578 284838, or see all FAQs.

I've never trained. Can I start?

Yes. Our beginners' classes are made for complete beginners. You'll warm up as a group, learn the technique of the day, and spar at the end when you're ready: in week one if you like, or watch and sit out if you're not. Nobody is ever forced to do anything they don't want to.

How does the £20 trial month work?

You get a full month of unlimited adult classes for £20. Start your trial here, then book your classes in the Gymdesk app. After your 30-day trial, membership continues automatically through Gymdesk at [Placeholder: roll-on price awaiting Reiss — £70 a month (2 classes a week) or £95 unlimited], unless you cancel before it ends.

Am I too old?

Almost certainly not. I started at 25 with no sports background, and both of my world titles came in the Masters divisions, for athletes over 30. You train at your own pace, rest whenever you need to and spar only when you're ready. [Placeholder: members who started in their 40s or later, with permission]

What should I wear?

Most of our classes are in the gi. For your first week we lend you one; after that, a loaner is £10 a class, or get your own. You can add an NSBJJ gi when you sign up and we'll fit you in person. Wear a t-shirt or rash guard under the gi and bring a water bottle. Kids can come to their free class in comfy sportswear.

What ages are the kids' classes?

Cubs are 4–7 and Juniors are 8–12. Your child's first class is free: book it online. After that, kids start our 4-month Intro Programme (gi included): £100 for the first month then £75 a month, or £300 paid in full, with 20% off for each brother or sister.

Is BJJ OK for women?

Absolutely. Women train in every class on the timetable, from beginners' sessions to advanced.

Do I need to book classes?

Yes, through the Gymdesk app or the online timetable. If a class is full, join the waiting list and you'll be told if a space opens up.
